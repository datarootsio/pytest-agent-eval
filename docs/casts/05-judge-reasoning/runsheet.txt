  READ ALL OF THIS BEFORE THE FIRST TAKE. This is the one cast that calls a real model.

  0. export OPENAI_API_KEY=sk-... IN THE SHELL YOU RUN rec.sh FROM, BEFORE you run it.
     Not on camera. rec.sh inherits your environment, so an already-exported key just
     works and is never filmed; typing the export inside the recording puts your key
     in a file you are about to upload to asciinema.org.

  1. It costs a fraction of a cent. Three judge calls on openai:gpt-4o-mini, one per
     run, on turn 2 only — turn 1 has no `expect`, so nothing judges it. Retakes are
     cheap; take as many as you need.

  2. The reasoning text WILL be different from your own previous take. It is a model
     writing a sentence, so no two takes read the same. That is fine and needs no
     follow-up: judges.md quotes no transcript, the recording is the only record of what
     the judge said. Just watch the take back and check the sentences actually make the
     page's point — that a judge names the clause of the rubric the reply broke.

  3. `-rP` on line 2 is not optional and is not a typo. pytest prints a report section
     for a FAILED test automatically, but NEVER for a passing one — and this test passes.
     Without `-rP` you get `1 passed` and no "LLM Eval" block at all, which is the one
     thing this page exists to show.
